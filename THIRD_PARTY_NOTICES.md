The model preparation process is recorded in `tools/prepare_weapon_models.py`.
The maintainer confirmed on 2026-11-02 that no license was supplied with the
models. Upstream authorship, source URL and redistribution permission remain
undocumented; see `assets/models/NOTICE.txt` (packaged as
`assets/licenses/Models.txt`). No model license or public-domain status is
asserted. Embedding the models does not change their terms.

The outlined Benga wordmark retains references to Philly Sans and is used for
this noncommercial project. The font's supplied non-commercial notice is in
`assets/fonts/philly_sans/non_commercial_license.txt`, included in packages as
`assets/licenses/Philly-Sans.txt`. The Philly Sans font binary is not embedded
or included in desktop packages. The project does not claim a commercial font
license or grant broader logo rights under GPL-2.0-only.
